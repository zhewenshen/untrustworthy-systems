About Me

Hi, I'm Zhewen. I'm a Software Engineer at Arista Networks.

I completed my Bachelor of Advanced Computer Science (Honours) at UNSW Sydney, graduating with Honours Class 1 and The University Medal. My honours thesis was supervised by Scientia Professor Gernot Heiser at the Trustworthy Systems group.

Before that, I worked with Dr Aditya Joshi and Dr Ruey-Cheng Chen on some fun NLP projects.

Research Interests

  • Operating systems: the seL4 microkernel and LionsOS
  • Programming languages for verified OS components (e.g. Pancake)
  • Natural language processing (in a previous life)

Projects

  • LionsOS (Research Intern, Trustworthy Systems, 2025): core migration and offlining for multicore management; a userspace webserver component; a proof-of-concept C-to-Pancake transpiler.
  • BAMBINO-LM (Research Intern, NLP, 2024): continual pre-training of small language models inspired by bilingual language acquisition in children (English ↔ Italian).
